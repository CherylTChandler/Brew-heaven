Our Philosophy
"Great coffee is grown, not made. We just try not to ruin it."

We travel to origin — the misty highlands of Coorg, the volcanic soils of Araku, the ancient forests of Ethiopia's Yirgacheffe zone, the fog-drenched slopes of Colombia's Huila department — to build direct relationships with farmers who share our obsession. Every bean is traceable to the exact farm, lot, and harvest season.

Our roasting philosophy is restrained: we roast to reveal the terroir, not to impose a house character. Each profile is dialled in over weeks to honour the unique personality of each origin — whether that's the winey brightness of a natural-processed Ethiopian or the clean, caramel sweetness of a washed Guatemalan.
